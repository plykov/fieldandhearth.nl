## 1. The path
Post -> reaction or comment -> delivery of the promised value (DM or reply) -> a light follow-up question -> a conversation if they reply -> an offer of a call when they describe a problem you solve.
Not every commenter is a lead. The goal is relationships with the right 5 to 10% and goodwill with the rest.

## 2. DM scripts (adapt to the user's voice)
**Delivery:** "Hi [name], thanks for the comment. Here is the [asset]: [link]. No strings. If it helps, I would be curious what you are working on with [topic]."
**Follow-up (3 to 5 days later, once):** "Did the [asset] help? If you are tackling [specific problem], happy to share how a client approached it."
**Offer of a call (only after they describe a fitting problem):** "That sounds like [problem I solve]. Want to talk it through for 20 minutes? No pitch, I will tell you honestly if I can help."
Rules: one follow-up unless they reply; never add them to a mailing list without saying so; stop if they do not respond. For deeper DM selling use `sell-by-chat`.

## 3. Handling volume
Prepare the asset and links before posting. Expect hundreds of comments from a big post and plan time for it. If DM limits block you, edit the post to add a direct link and say why. Reply to as many comments as practical, since replies also lift the post.

## 4. 90-day plan
- **Weeks 1-2:** offer, buyer, pillars, archetype, proof inventory, profile aligned (use `linkedin-profile-audit`).
- **Weeks 3-6:** 3 to 4 posts a week across pillars; one comment-to-get post in week 4 with the asset ready.
- **Weeks 7-10:** double down on the top two formats; add one case study with a figure and a client's permission.
- **Weeks 11-13:** review leads, calls and wins; retire weak pillars; draft the next quarter.

## 5. What to track
Relevant conversations started, calls booked, deals won and their source post; profile views from the target buyer; not follower count alone.
