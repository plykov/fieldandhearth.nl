# AI Agent Sales Team Playbook (SaaStr pattern)

Based on the Origin Notes SaaStr teardown (2026), paraphrased. All results are self-reported by SaaStr and unaudited: 3 humans plus about 21 agents; sales from 10 SDRs/AEs to 1.25 humans; $4.8M agent-sourced pipeline and $2.4M closed-won by Feb 2026; an inbound agent handled 442,000 chats and booked 614 meetings in one event cycle; B leads ~ $500K a year; a win-back campaign to ~1,000 ghosted leads saw 72% opens and 10%+ replies; one agent closed a $70,000 sponsorship alone. The same founder later wrote that agents cannot close a real deal of any size on their own. Treat results as context, not targets.

Companions: `gtm-tactic-library`, `content-engine-audience-business`, `transactional-sales-call-process`, `sell-by-chat`, `jmm-outbound-prospecting`, `meddicc-deal-qualification`, `sales-funnel-time-and-opportunity-scoring`.

## Workflow
1. **Readiness check** (`references/agent-pilot.md`): proven human playbook, a rep worth copying, lead volume, time and budget; not a fit if you close ~40 large deals a year or never had a good rep.
2. **Pick one job per agent** (inbound chat, warm outbound, net-new accounts, website visitors and ghosted prospects, renewals) and start with B leads that humans skip.
3. **Capture the best rep's workflow and script**; define handoff to humans and what an agent may never do (pricing exceptions, legal terms, negotiation).
4. **Train and review daily**: about 30 days of daily training per agent; two people at 15-20 hours a week each; log outputs; fix hallucinations; keep texture and honesty in messages.
5. **Coordinate**: a layer that routes leads and avoids duplicate or conflicting contact.
6. **Measure**: meetings booked, qualified pipeline, closed-won, win-back rates, response quality audits, escalation rate, cost per meeting, human hours spent.
7. **Staff the humans** on negotiation, large and unknown-buyer deals, relationship work.
8. **Review quarterly**; shut down agents that fail quality audits.

## Output template
Readiness verdict | agent roster with one job each | script and guardrail spec | training and review schedule | handoff rules | metrics and thresholds | budget | risks (compliance, brand, rep morale).

## Guardrails
- Disclose that a buyer is talking to an AI where required by law or platform rules; follow consent, anti-spam and do-not-contact laws.
- Don't let agents invent product claims, pricing or references; log and audit.
- Plan for staff impact honestly; one rep in the case quit the day agent activity summaries began.
- Confidential lead data needs privacy and security review before agent access.
