# Body structures and a sixth format

From a second analysis (850 top-decile posts). Use with the five templates in `templates.md`.

## Sixth format: inspirational or mindset insight
Two to six lines, punchy, often epigrammatic. One principle the audience already feels but has not said. Example shape: "Winning requires reps." then "There is one way to make up for a skill gap: volume." Works when the idea is true, compact and new in its phrasing. Pair it with one line of proof from your own life so it does not float. Pick it for low-effort frequency days; it carries little teaching value alone.

## Hook-to-payoff contract
Count what the hook promised (four steps, a story, a "why") and make the body deliver exactly that. Posts that tease and then give generic content feel like bait-and-switch and lose trust, which matters most when the goal is to attract clients.

## Body shapes that recur
- **Challenge, then results:** labelled blocks such as BACKGROUND, THE CHALLENGE, THE RESULTS, with figures in bullets. Good for experiments and case studies; sharing the bad as well as the good ("the good, the bad, the ugly") signals transparency.
- **Story then lesson:** quick narrative, then "what I took from it".
- **Numbered steps with an example each.** Verb-first, one or two lines.
- **Problem, agitate, solution:** state the pain, sharpen the stakes, give the way out.
- **Dialogue snippets:** "Client: ... Me: ..." for scenarios the audience recognizes.

## Formatting
One to two sentences per paragraph, blank lines between, caps for section labels, a few arrows or checkmarks as bullets, a down-pointing cue before key content. Edit hard: cut filler and jargon, and re-read the first three lines last.

## Give real substance
Top posts hand over something non-obvious (a tactic, a framework, a number), which earns trust through generosity. Avoid padding a thin idea into a long post.

## Trust moves inside the body
- Show a result with its figure and timeframe, when true.
- Admit a mistake you made; vulnerability with a lesson builds credibility.
- Name your own method if you have one (for example "my three-phase framework"), so the idea is memorable and attributable. Only brand a method you actually use.
