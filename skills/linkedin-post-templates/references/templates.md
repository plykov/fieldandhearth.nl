# The five LinkedIn post templates

Bracketed items are for the user's real details.

## Contents
1. Personal story with lesson
2. How-to educational
3. Listicle of insights
4. Contrarian opinion
5. Lead magnet offer
6. Combining and adapting

## 1. Personal story with lesson
**Use when:** you have a real experience that taught something your audience needs.
**Arc:** hook, context, conflict, resolution, lesson, question.

- **Line 1 (hook):** foreshadow the lesson or the dramatic moment. "I thought I had [skill] figured out. I was wrong."
- **Line 2 (rehook):** stakes. "[Short context], and I nearly [lost/failed] [something]."
- **Line 3:** a colon promise. "Here is what happened:"
- **Scene:** "I was [time] into [role/project] when [challenge hit]." Add one concrete or sensory detail.
- **Struggle:** what you felt, what you tried, what failed. Honest vulnerability helps trust.
- **Turning point:** the insight or advice that changed the approach.
- **Result:** "Within [time], [outcome]. We went from [bad] to [better]." Give a number if true.
- **Lesson:** one clear sentence, generalized so readers can use it.
- **Question:** "Have you faced something like this? What did you learn?"

## 2. How-to educational
**Use when:** you can teach a method; strong for consultants and service providers showing expertise.
**Arc:** result hook, credibility line, numbered steps, close, question.

- **Hook:** "How to [result] in [time] without [obstacle]."
- **Credibility line:** "As a [role], I used these [n] steps to [result]."
- **Steps (3 to 7):** each as "1. [Action title]: [one or two sentences, with a result or example]." Start with verbs. Include a number from your experience where you truly have one.
- **Close:** "These steps took us from [before] to [after]. You can do it one step at a time."
- **Question:** "Which step would help you most? Share your own go-to."

## 3. Listicle of insights
**Use when:** you have several short lessons or myths from experience.
**Arc:** numbered hook, one line of context, 5 to 10 items, wrap-up, question.

- **Hook:** "[n] lessons I learned about [topic] in [years]." or "[n] myths about [topic] I had to unlearn."
- **Context:** "When I started, I believed [assumption]. [Time] later, here is the reality:"
- **Items:** a short bold-ish claim, then one sentence of why or an example. Items of one or two lines each. "Listening beats having all the answers."
- **Wrap-up:** "I am still learning, but these have changed how I [do topic]."
- **Question:** "Which one resonates? What would you add?"
Five to seven items is the comfortable range. Past ten, split into two posts.

## 4. Contrarian opinion
**Use when:** you hold a considered view against a common belief, ideally tied to a current conversation.
**Arc:** provocative hook, common view, your argument with evidence, new perspective, discussion invite.

- **Hook:** "[Common belief] is wrong. Here is why." or "[Concept A] is not [Concept B]."
- **Set-up:** "Many people assume [belief]. I did too. Then [what you saw]."
- **Argument:** a short story or two or three reasons, plus a stat or example. Acknowledge nuance.
- **New perspective:** state your principle and why it works better.
- **Invite:** "Do you agree? What has been your experience?"
Target ideas, not people. Offer an alternative, not just a complaint. Stay in the comments and keep disagreement respectful.

## 5. Lead magnet offer
**Use when:** you have a free asset worth having and the capacity to send it to every requester.
**Arc:** pain hook, what you made, what is inside, how to get it, optional deadline, tag nudge.

- **Hook:** "Struggling with [pain]? I made something for you."
- **Offer:** "I just finished a [format] that [benefit]."
- **Inside:** 3 to 5 bullets of concrete contents, then one line on the outcome.
- **How to get it:** "Comment [word] and I will send it over." The link goes by DM or first comment, not in the body.
- **Optional deadline:** only if real, such as a date you will stop sending.
- **Tag nudge:** "Know someone who needs this?"
Rules: the asset must be genuinely useful; deliver to everyone promptly; be honest if you will follow up afterward (for example, an email opt-in) so the exchange is not a surprise; do not use it every week. For building the asset itself, use `lead-magnet-playbook`.

## 6. Combining and adapting
- Story plus list: finish a story with three lessons.
- Opinion plus story: ground the contrarian claim in one real episode.
- Story plus offer: end a story with the resource that came from it.
- Add the trending topic as a variable in the hook of any template, but keep your own insight, since reporting news alone adds nothing.
- Carousel (swipeable PDF): usually a listicle or how-to in slides; the cover slide needs the same hook discipline.
- Fit to the user's niche: the structure is universal, the examples must be theirs.
