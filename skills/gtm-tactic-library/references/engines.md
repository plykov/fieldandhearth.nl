# Engines in the teardown series (paraphrased)
Series thesis (the author's): the best GTM engines turn something people already want into the distribution itself.

| Engine | Companies | Core mechanism | Choose when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bottom-up to enterprise | Replit, Hugging Face, Supabase, others | individuals adopt, teams spread, someone requests enterprise, IT signs | one person gets value alone in a day and an enterprise layer exists |
| Product output as distribution | Gamma, Lovable | what users make carries the brand and pulls the next user | output is proud-to-share |
| Agent/software as buyer | Supabase | other products and AI agents choose and set up your product | product is operable without a person |
| Trust | Rogo, Harvey | verifiable output, marquee names, insiders, peer proof | cost of error is high; prestige hierarchy |
| Content/audience loop | Lenny, SaaStr | content to audience to products stacked on the audience | years of credible publishing available |
| Open platform / scoreboard | Hugging Face, Vercel, OpenRouter | free on-ramp, neutral hub, network effects, signal others cite | early to an emerging field; can stay neutral |
| Scarcity and seeding | Instinct (and Superhuman, Robinhood) | gated access, seeded authorities, a repeated message | real demand behind the gate; product survives scrutiny |
| Agent-run sales | SaaStr | one-job agents on a proven playbook | working playbook and lead volume |
| Narrative/fear (recognition only) | frontier AI labs per the author | unmeasurable threat reprices valuation | do not copy; learn to spot it |

Supporting plays recurring across the series: founder-led social (12+ companies), freemium plus usage pricing (9+), launch campaigns, marketplaces and partners, micro-influencers.
Selection rule of thumb: pick the engine whose preconditions you already meet, then add at most two supports. Engines differ because preconditions differ (the author contrasts Rogo's trust engine with Cursor's product-superiority engine).
