# AI and Dev-Tool Startup GTM Tactics

From the Origin Notes teardown of Replit (2026), paraphrased. For each tactic: what it is, a number to calibrate (company-reported or estimated), the precondition, and when not to copy. The fifth tactic, bottom-up to enterprise, has its own skill: `bottom-up-to-enterprise-gtm`. Use `gtm-teardown-method` to analyse other companies in the same format.

Companion skills: `marketing-sales-and-positioning`, `position-b2b-tech`, `business-strategy-and-startups`, `social-media-engagement-tactics`.

## Workflow
1. **State the product, buyer and constraint** (model costs, trust, complexity, time to value).
2. **Screen tactics** with the table in `references/tactics.md`: check each precondition honestly with evidence, and the "don't copy if" line.
3. **Pick one or two** to run now; sequence others later.
4. **Define leading metrics** per tactic (first-day activation, shares and clips, margin per heavy user, bill-shock complaints, inbound from founder content, marketplace-sourced deals).
5. **Plan the failure modes** (cost per request, unpredictable bills, founder becoming the story, listing without demand).
6. Output the plan and a "not now" list.

## Output template
Tactic scorecard (precondition met? evidence) | chosen tactics with 30/60/90-day actions | metrics and thresholds | risks and mitigations | what would make us change course.

## Guardrails
- Treat figures as dated, mostly company-reported; do not imply similar results.
- Don't recommend manufactured virality, fake demos or undisclosed paid promotion; the pattern works when the real output is remarkable.
- Pricing must be transparent about what a request costs; avoid designs that trap users into surprise bills.
- Public fights and personal brand carry reputational risk; advise judgment and legal review where relevant.
