# Invite-Only Launch and Seeding (Instinct pattern, with guardrails)

Based on the Origin Notes Instinct teardown (2026), paraphrased. Reported: invite-only personal AI assistant, 100K+ users with no public launch and no revenue, valuation repriced from about $50M to $2.5B in months and a $10B round in talks; users, retention and task completion not independently verified. The author calls it the first teardown where the playbook moved the valuation rather than revenue.

Companions: `gtm-tactic-library`, `gtm-teardown-method`, `social-media-engagement-tactics`, `likable-copywriting`, `persuasive-writing-and-storytelling`.

## Workflow
1. **Test readiness**: real demand behind the gate? product good enough to survive scrutiny from influential users (their worst reviews go to your loudest voices)?
2. **Design access**: waitlist plus member and expert invites; throttle by capacity; make access earned or vouched; avoid paid acquisition; keep it gated only as long as it serves quality.
3. **Seed deliberately**: choose a small set of credible operators who will use it for real; give them real work to do with it.
4. **Give them a clear, true frame**: a simple label and concrete completed outcomes they actually experienced; let their own words differ.
5. **Require disclosure**: endorsers state investments, advisory roles, free access or commissions; do not supply scripted testimonials without disclosure.
6. **Plan capacity and safety**: compute limits, degraded response times, honest status messages; test failure modes (CAPTCHA, two-factor, ticketing crashes).
7. **Review terms and data access**: avoid sweeping licences over user content; set training opt-outs; honour disconnects and deletions; document what the agent may do on a user's behalf.
8. **Measure real traction**: activation, retention, task completion, willingness to pay; do not treat valuation or buzz as proof.

## Output template
Readiness verdict | gating and capacity plan | seeding list and onboarding | message frame with true outcomes | disclosure rules | safety/terms review | traction metrics | exit from scarcity plan.

## Guardrails
- Do not fabricate demand, fake waitlist counts, buy testimonials, or coordinate undisclosed endorsements; follow advertising and endorsement disclosure rules (for example FTC-style rules) in your jurisdiction.
- Scarcity around something nobody wants is just a small user base with extra steps.
- Agent products with broad account access carry real legal and security exposure; be transparent, minimise access, and let users revoke it.
