# Metrics loop

## Track per post
Date and time, format, hook family, topic, length (characters), media (none/image/carousel), impressions, reactions, comments, shares, profile views gained, DMs or leads started, first-hour comments.

## Compute
- **Engagement rate** = (reactions + comments + shares) / impressions. The source puts typical rates around 2 to 3%; treat that as a rough anchor, then compare against your own median.
- **Comment ratio** = comments / reactions. Posts with a higher ratio show conversation.
- **Lead rate** = relevant conversations started / impressions. This is the number that connects to the business.

## Weekly routine (20 minutes)
1. Log last week's posts.
2. Sort by engagement rate and by lead rate. They are often different winners.
3. Note what the top two share (format, hook, topic, time) and what the bottom two share.
4. Pick one variable to test next week (a hook family, a posting slot, a format). Change only that.
5. Write down the prediction so you learn from being wrong.

## Monthly review
- Which formats and topics bring the right people?
- Is the baseline impression count rising?
- Are you hitting the cadence you can sustain? If not, cut the target, not the quality.
- Repost a top performer in updated form after a few months; most of the audience has not seen it.

## Targets (suggestions, not promises)
Move engagement rate up gradually (for example from 2% toward 4%) by improving hooks and calls to action. Prefer rising profile views and inbound messages over vanity follower counts.
