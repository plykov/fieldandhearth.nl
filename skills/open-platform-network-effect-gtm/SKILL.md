# Open Platform and Network-Effect GTM (Hugging Face pattern)

Based on the Origin Notes Hugging Face teardown (2026), paraphrased. Reported: founded 2016, pivoted 2019, monetizing from 2021, ~$70M ARR 2023, $100M+ by mid-2026 (CEO), a $4.5B valuation in 2023, and an announced $12.9B acquisition by Nvidia (Sept 2026, pending); the Hub hosts 3M+ models for 18M+ developers and 200K companies (acquirer's figures). Estimates conflict; treat the curve as directional.

Companions: `gtm-tactic-library`, `agent-ready-product-distribution`, `bottom-up-to-enterprise-gtm`, `business-strategy-and-startups`.

## Workflow
1. **Find the friction** a free tool can remove for a field about to produce a flood of shareable work.
2. **Design the on-ramp**: open library/tool that becomes the default and leads users to your hub.
3. **Host and stay neutral**: free publishing of models/datasets/demos; no house favourites; governance that signals neutrality.
4. **Delay money deliberately**: fund the wait; start monetizing with small paid features and compute; avoid taxing contributors.
5. **Add the enterprise layer** once teams use you: private hubs, on-prem, security, audit logs, compliance.
6. **Build trusted signals**: downloads, trending, leaderboards; protect integrity; retire signals that get gamed.
7. **Replay in adjacent fields** early (the case moved to robotics: library plus datasets).
8. **Plan for the funnel position**: you win discovery and comparison; cloud providers may capture production hosting; know where revenue will actually come from.
9. **Stress-test neutrality** against ownership change, major partners and competitors.

## Output template
Field and friction | on-ramp spec | neutrality commitments | monetization sequence and runway | enterprise layer | signal/scoreboard design and anti-gaming | next-field options | funnel-position and acquisition risks.

## Guardrails
- Neutrality is the asset; don't promise what ownership or investors may later change.
- Moderate hosted content for safety, licence and rights issues; open does not mean unmoderated.
- Treat valuations and acquisition numbers as reported, not verified.
