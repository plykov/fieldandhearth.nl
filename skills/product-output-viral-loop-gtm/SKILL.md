# Product-Output Viral Loop (Gamma pattern)

Based on the Origin Notes Gamma teardown (2026), paraphrased. Reported by the company and press: $100M+ ARR (Nov 2025) with about 50 employees and about $23M raised before the Series B, profitable since 2023, 600K+ paying, 1M+ artifacts a day, $2.1B valuation. User counts differ across sources. Illustrative only.

Companions: `gtm-tactic-library`, `ai-startup-gtm-tactics`, `marketing-sales-and-positioning`, `social-media-engagement-tactics`.

## Workflow
1. **Check the output**: is it shared by default (decks, links, reports) and good enough to be proud of? Screenshot-worthy?
2. **Design the badge-to-revenue link**: a visible brand mark on free outputs; removing it is the first reason to pay; add credits, teams, analytics, custom domain as higher tiers.
3. **Choose the buyer**: define the professional with urgency and budget; resist "anyone who wants to tell a story" positioning (the Tome lesson, `references/gamma-playbook.md`).
4. **Engineer time to wow**: a complete, designed draft from one prompt; target an aha within 30 seconds that makes someone tell a friend unprompted.
5. **Founder-first content**: make the demo yourself, onboard creators personally, learn what lands.
6. **Scale with small creators**: roughly 70% of creator spend on 10K-100K follower creators, performance-tested, only where the product demos in 30 seconds.
7. **Plan for copycats**: incumbents bundle the feature; moat candidates are format (web-native link, collaboration, analytics), taste and speed.

## Output template
Output audit | badge/tier design | buyer definition and anti-persona | first-30-seconds spec | founder content plan | creator test plan with kill criteria | competitive moat hypothesis | metrics (shares per output, badge impressions, free-to-paid conversion, churn).

## Guardrails
- Don't spam or dark-pattern the badge (e.g. hiding it in ways that deceive); disclose paid creator partnerships.
- Treat the case as survivorship-biased; many similar startups failed.
- Don't promise virality; it needs shareable output and real urgency.
