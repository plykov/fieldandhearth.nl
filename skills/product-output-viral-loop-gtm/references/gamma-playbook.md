# Gamma playbook details (paraphrased)
Product: turns a sentence into a finished deck, document or simple website; free users generate and share with a "Made with Gamma" badge; paid plans ($8 Plus, $18 Pro, $100 Ultra at the time) remove the badge, raise credits, add teams, analytics, branding, custom domain.
## Plays (play / number / precondition / don't copy if)
1. **Turn every output into an ad**: 1M+ branded pieces a day; over half of subscriber growth attributed to word of mouth. Precondition: output people are proud or impressed enough to share. Don't copy if output is not screenshot-worthy.
2. **Focus on the target buyer**: positioned for professionals with a deadline and a budget; ~600K paying and profitable since 2023, versus Tome (raised $81M, 20M+ users, never above $4M ARR, pivoted). Don't copy if your only honest positioning is mass consumer with no willingness to pay (then virality is the business and you monetise otherwise, at Meta's scale).
3. **First 30 seconds magical**: before AI 95% of users bounced off a blank canvas; one-prompt full draft fixed it; 60K to 3M users in three months after the March 2023 AI relaunch. Don't copy if value shows only after setup or weeks.
4. **Become the creator yourself**: the founder made the demo content and onboarded creators over video calls. Don't copy if the founder won't be in the channel.
5. **Pay micro-influencers**: ~70% of creator spend on 10K-100K followers; 1,000+ activated.
## Risks and caveats
Feature copied by Microsoft, Google and Canva inside suites; consumer churn; "AI makes my deck" becoming a checkbox; head start from a year of onboarding work; survivorship bias.
