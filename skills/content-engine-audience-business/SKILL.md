# Content Engine and Audience Business (Lenny + SaaStr patterns)

Based on two Origin Notes teardowns (2026), paraphrased. Figures are company-reported or outside estimates: Lenny's newsletter 1.2M+ free subscribers, podcast channel 600K+ subscribers, mid-seven-figure operating revenue (outside estimate), no full-time employees; SaaStr rebuilt from 20+ staff to 3 humans plus about 21 agents with revenue near $10M+ (company), YouTube 138K to 200K subscribers in six months. Illustrative only.

Companions: `gtm-tactic-library`, `persuasive-writing-and-storytelling`, `social-media-strategy-planner`, `niching-physics`, `tribe-positioning`, `lead-magnet-playbook`, `email-blueprint`, `ai-agent-sales-team-playbook`.

## Workflow
1. **Check the preconditions** (`references/preconditions.md`): credible story, network/runway, early niche, savings, years of cadence. Be honest about what you lack and plan substitutes.
2. **Pick the home audience** that has no default publication yet, and a tentpole plan (a few deep pieces over steady volume).
3. **Design the loop**: one source act (guest/recording/idea) to many formats; free thread or post that routes to gated content; guests booked as content, distributor and relationship.
4. **Build distribution partners**: platform recommendation features, cross-promotion, podcasts, creators.
5. **Stack products on the audience**: sponsorship, community, courses, job board, bundles; sequence them after the audience exists; consider partner-tool bundles where partners benefit.
6. **Make the archive programmable**: AI-ready files, MCP server, full set for paid and a slice for free, builder challenges, optional founder clone.
7. **If B2B**: publish your operation (numbers, costs, failures), become a transparent reference customer (with disclosure), write research-stage content for SEO/AEO, hold a founder voice.
8. **Protect cadence and the credibility input**: write the rest policy, keep close to real work so authority isn't spent without renewal.

## Output template
Preconditions audit | audience and positioning | loop diagram (source, formats, funnel) | tentpole calendar | product stack with order and metrics | AI-programmability plan | B2B proof plan with disclosures | cadence and burnout plan | revenue model assumptions flagged as estimates.

## Guardrails
- Disclose sponsorships, affiliate links and vendor relationships; reviews must stay honest, including failures.
- Don't fabricate numbers or scrape others' content; license training data and respect privacy for transcripts and community content.
- Revenue figures here are estimates; never present them as the creator's confirmed earnings.
